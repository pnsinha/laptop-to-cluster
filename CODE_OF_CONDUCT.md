# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free project for people of every background, identity, experience level, and access need.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, respect privacy and consent, use inclusive language, accept correction, and focus technical criticism on the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `parmanandsinha@gmail.com`; do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. The maintainer will acknowledge reports when practical, protect confidentiality to the extent possible, investigate conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate actions ranging from correction through temporary or permanent exclusion.

For a concern involving the maintainer, use the BSSw community contact published at https://bssw.io/contact as the independent alternative channel.
