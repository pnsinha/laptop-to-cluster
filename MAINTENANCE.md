# Maintenance and content review

## Ownership

Parmanand Sinha is the project maintainer and publication owner in an individual 2026 BSSw Fellowship capacity. University of Chicago affiliation does not make the institution the operator or support provider for this project.

## Review expectations

Technical content is reviewed against its documented review date, applicable environment, scheduler/runtime versions, and evidence. Editorial content is checked for authority, links, accessibility, and stable URLs. License/provenance declarations and recovery contacts are checked for every release candidate.

## Workflow

Issues are triaged by affected artifact and severity. Pull requests receive the scope-aware review described in `CONTRIBUTING.md`; CI validates build, tests, accessibility baseline, repository policy, and release readiness. Material technical changes require updated applicability/evidence before a validated claim is published.

## Response expectations

This is a fellowship resource, not a staffed service desk. Reports are acknowledged and prioritized when maintainer availability permits; security and site-wide accessibility blockers take precedence. No fixed response or resolution time is guaranteed.

## Post-fellowship path

Before active maintenance ends, the site will publish a final maintenance date, archive status, v1.0 reconstruction instructions, and reuse path. Open licenses continue to permit adaptation. Current status and alternative contact behavior are maintained in `SUPPORT.md` and `RECOVERY.md`.
